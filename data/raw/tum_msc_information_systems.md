## Application periods
The M.Sc. Information Systems at TUM starts in the summer or winter semester. For the winter semester applications are accepted from 1 February to 31 May; for the summer semester from 1 October to 30 November.

## Admission process
Admission is a two-stage process in which TUM assesses the applicant against the special qualitative requirements of the program. Applicants submit a statement of reasons explaining why they chose the program and TUM specifically. The admission office checks the uploaded documents for completeness and then forwards the application to the admission committee, which admits, rejects or invites the applicant to the second stage of the aptitude assessment.

## International applicants
Documents must be checked against TUM's notarization requirements, especially translations. A VPD from uni-assist alone does not count as an application. TUM charges tuition fees to non-EU international students who newly enrolled from winter semester 2024/25.

## Academic foundations
The captured text lists foundations in informatics and mathematics that are checked, such as Introduction to Informatics, Linear Algebra for Informatics and Analysis for Informatics. Other programs may not qualify for this master's; applicants should compare their bachelor's curriculum with the requirements on the official page.
