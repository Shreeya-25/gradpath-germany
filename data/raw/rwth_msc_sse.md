## Overview
The Software Systems Engineering (SSE) master at the Faculty of Computer Science of RWTH Aachen University is a computer science program with a strong focus on software engineering. It is a two-year, four-semester program aimed especially but not only at international students, and English skills are an admission requirement.

## Requirements and deadline
Applicants need a bachelor's degree in computer science or a closely related field from an internationally recognized university. The application for admission deadline is 1 March for the following winter term of that year. This applies to many but not all students, so applicants should read the details on the application page and the FAQ before applying.
