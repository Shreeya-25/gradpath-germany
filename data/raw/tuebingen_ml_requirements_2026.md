## Admission requirements
Applicants need a bachelor's degree with a standard period of study of at least six semesters and at least 180 ECTS credits in a subject related to machine learning, such as computer science. The FAQ states that the required skills and the grade threshold are hard requirements: an applicant with a bachelor's grade worse than 2.0 on the German scale is not eligible, even with a lot of machine learning experience, and missing requirements cannot be fixed by taking extra courses in Tuebingen.

## Deadline
The application deadline for the Machine Learning master was 30 April 2026. Applications are made online and opened from February 2026. The deadline for the Oct 2027 intake was not yet published in the captured page; applicants should check the page from February 2027.

## English proof and APS
Applicants need proof of English proficiency. Applicants with a bachelor's degree from a university in Australia, Canada, Ireland, New Zealand, the UK or the USA are the exception to the English test rule. Questions about the requirements can be sent to ml-master@inf.uni-tuebingen.de.
