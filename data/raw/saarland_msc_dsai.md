## Overview
The M.Sc. in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence at Saarland University in Saarbruecken lasts 4 semesters (120 ECTS) and is taught entirely in English. Professors from the Department of Computer Science and the Department of Language Science and Technology, plus scientists from research centres on campus, teach in the program. An industrial internship and a master internship in a research group can be part of the electives. International applications are welcome and no German is required for admission.

## Deadlines (sources disagree)
The DAAD mirror lists an early deadline of 15 May, with notification by July for positive decisions, and a normal deadline of 15 November. A StudyBuddy listing shows the winter intake deadline as 15 May and the summer intake deadline as 15 November. A third listing (gradgermany) assigns 15 November to the winter intake and 15 May to the summer intake, which conflicts with the other two. Because the sources disagree, confirm the deadline for the winter 2027 intake on the official Saarland University page before planning.

## Costs and uni-assist
No tuition fees are charged, including for non-EU students. The semester contribution is listed as about 300 EUR by one source and 362 EUR by another. One listing says uni-assist is not required. The program is not locally admission-restricted according to a listing.
