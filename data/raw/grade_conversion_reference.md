## Modified Bavarian formula
German universities usually convert foreign grades with the modified Bavarian formula: German grade = 1 + 3 x (N_max - N_d) / (N_max - N_min), where N_max is the best possible grade, N_min is the lowest passing grade and N_d is the applicant's grade. In Germany 1.0 is the best grade and 4.0 is the lowest pass, so lower is better. The result is an estimate; the university or uni-assist makes the official conversion and may use different N_max and N_min values.

## Indian CGPA on a 10-point scale
A CGPA can be converted either directly on the 10-point scale or through a percentage. Many Indian universities, including VTU, publish a CGPA-to-percentage formula; for VTU it is commonly given as percentage = (CGPA - 0.75) x 10. Because the direct and percentage methods can give noticeably different German grades, applicants near a grade cut-off should ask the university which method it uses.
