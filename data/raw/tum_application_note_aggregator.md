## TUM application guide for Indian students (third-party, unverified)
This third-party guide, marked as last verified in February 2026, says TUM does not accept applications directly via uni-assist, that the application portal is TUMonline, and that the winter deadline of 31 May is earlier than at most German public universities, which commonly allow applications until 15 July. It also describes a 100-point aptitude assessment in which a stage 2 score of 70 or more secures admission. This scoring claim does not appear on the official TUM Informatics page, so treat it as unverified and check the official program page, since procedure details differ by program.
