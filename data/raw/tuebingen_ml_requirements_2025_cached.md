## Admission requirements (older version of the page, superseded)
An older cached version of the Tuebingen requirements page asks for a bachelor's degree related to machine learning and at least 27 credit points (CP) of mathematics courses. It states that an applicant with a grade worse than 2.3 on the German scale is not eligible, and that formal requirements cannot be fulfilled afterwards with additional courses in Tuebingen. The deadline listed there was 30 April 2025. This version conflicts with the newer 2026 page, which uses a 2.0 threshold, so prefer the 2026 version.

## APS certificate
Applicants with a bachelor's degree from China, India or Vietnam must provide an APS certificate. It is not required if, after the bachelor's in one of those countries, the applicant obtained a master's degree from another country. Decisions normally arrive within about four weeks after the application deadline.
