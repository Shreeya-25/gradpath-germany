## Program overview
The international M.Sc. Machine Learning at the University of Tuebingen is research-oriented. It has four study areas: Foundations of Machine Learning, Diverse Topics in Machine Learning, General Computer Science and Expanded Perspectives. Apart from a few mandatory courses, students choose subjects by interest, and the program cannot be combined with a minor subject. Recommended first-semester modules are Mathematics for Machine Learning and Data Literacy, followed by Statistical Learning, Probabilistic Inference and Learning, and Deep Learning.

## Admission status
The English program page says admission is not admission-restricted but all applicants, including those with a bachelor's in computer science, must submit a formal application. The German listing and a third-party listing describe the program as restricted admission, so the sources are inconsistent. Always follow the current requirements page.
