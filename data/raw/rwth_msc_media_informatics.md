## Qualification requirements (aggregator)
This aggregator listing says applicants need a bachelor's degree in computer science, computer engineering, informatics or a closely related discipline. If the first degree is in another subject, the background in computer science, mathematics and engineering must still be strong, otherwise a bridging course is required. It lists at least 28 credit points (CP) in applied computer science, at least 18 CP in computer engineering (including 6 CP in computer and electrical engineering) and at least 18 CP in theoretical computer science.

## GRE, language and deadlines
The listing states that the GRE General Test is mandatory. English proficiency can also be proven by the RWTH language centre placement test at level B2. The winter semester deadline is listed as 1 March or 15 July depending on the applicant's personal deadline. This is a third-party listing, so verify requirements on the official RWTH page.
