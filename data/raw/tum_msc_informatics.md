## Overview
The M.Sc. Informatics at TU Munich (TUM) is a research-oriented degree that builds on computer science fundamentals from the bachelor. It takes 4 semesters and 120 ECTS credits, is taught in English, is based mainly in Garching, and can be studied full-time or part-time. Students can start in the summer or winter semester and specialise in one of eight computer science areas.

## Application periods and deadlines
For the winter semester the compulsory application period runs from 1 February to 31 May. Applicants who need a visa are recommended to apply between 1 February and 31 March. For the summer semester the compulsory period is 1 October to 30 November, with 1 to 31 October recommended for visa applicants. Missing documents cannot be submitted after the official deadline. Lectures start in October (winter) and April (summer). The on-campus aptitude test for winter 2026/27 applicants was on 20 August 2026; tests for winter applicants take place between July and September.

## Qualification requirements
Applicants need a bachelor's degree in Informatics or Computer Science that is equivalent to the B.Sc. Informatics taught at TUM. Other degree programs do not qualify for this master's. Proof of sufficient English proficiency is also required. The assessment has two stages: the admission committee first reviews the documents and decides to admit, reject, or invite the applicant to the second stage, which is a written test.

## Required documents
A statement of reasons (maximum 2 pages), a CV, a scientific essay of about 1,000 words, the form Analysis of the Curriculum, the bachelor's certificate with course list and grades or official transcript, the curriculum with module descriptions of the bachelor's degree (very important for checking qualification), proof of English proficiency, a passport photo and a passport copy. Documents not in German or English must be translated and notarized by a sworn translator. Certificates from additional training, internships or research competitions are optional but helpful.

## Applicants from India: GRE, GATE, APS and uni-assist
Applicants with a bachelor's degree from India, Bangladesh, China, Iran or Pakistan must submit a GRE General Test score or an Indian GATE score. Minimum GRE scores are Quantitative Reasoning 164 and Analytical Writing 4.0; the verbal score is no longer taken into account; lower scores are not accepted. The ETS institution code is 7806 and the department code is 5199. Alternatively a GATE Computer Science scorecard meeting the minimum qualifying score of that year is accepted. Applicants with a bachelor's degree from India, China or Vietnam must also submit an APS certificate from the German Evaluation Centre. Everyone whose bachelor's was not obtained in Germany must request a preliminary documentation (VPD) from uni-assist in addition to the TUM application; a VPD alone is not an application. Applications are submitted online through TUMonline; certified copies are needed only after admission.

## Written test (stage 2)
The written test lasts 90 minutes with roughly 20 to 30 questions in English. It covers general foundations (max 12 points), mathematical foundations (max 13 points: stochastics, combinatorics, calculus, linear algebra, numerical programming), theory of computation (max 13 points: automata, complexity, algorithms and data structures, verification) and practical foundations of informatics (max 13 points: databases, computer architecture, programming in Java).

## Essay and statement of reasons
The scientific essay must be about 1,000 words in English, with proper citations, on one of five topics such as the role of artificial intelligence in future technology, the influence of social networks, Big Data platforms, whether computers can think, or the importance of digitalization. The statement of reasons must explain the applicant's specific qualification and commitment for this master's, for example internships, research papers or stays abroad.

## Tuition and fees
TUM charges tuition fees to international students from non-EU countries who newly enrolled from winter semester 2024/25. The exact amount is on TUM's tuition page. Third-party aggregator sites quote roughly 1,500 EUR or more per semester; confirm on the official fee page.

## Enrollment and postponing
Enrollment requires proof of the completed bachelor's degree by the enrollment deadline, with no exceptions. Admitted students who cannot enroll can postpone only by reapplying within the next official deadline; there is no automatic notification.
